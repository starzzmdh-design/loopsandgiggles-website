Our story

Every plushie is crocheted by hand with love, care, and a whole lot of yarn. From ocean critters to soaring stitches, each little creature is one of a kind — just like the person who takes it home.

We currently sell in person at local craft fairs. Browse the gallery below to see what we've been making, then come say hi at our next event!
